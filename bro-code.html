On Brotherhood

The Bro Code is a collection of principles governing the sacred bond between Bros. It exists to preserve loyalty, respect, trust, and the traditions of brotherhood. These rules are not merely suggestions. They are a way of life.

Chapter I — The Foundation

Article 001

Bros Before Everything

A Bro shall never abandon another Bro when his presence is genuinely needed. Brotherhood requires loyalty, not convenience.

Article 002

The Sacred Bond

A Bro shall treat the bond between Bros with respect. Trust, once given, shall never be treated as a weakness.

Article 003

Honor Among Bros

A Bro shall not knowingly humiliate, betray, or deliberately undermine another Bro.

Chapter II — Loyalty & Trust

Article 004

The Bro's Secret

A secret entrusted to a Bro shall remain confidential, unless keeping it would put someone in danger.

Article 005

The Rule of Support

A Bro shall support another Bro through difficult times. Advice may be offered, but judgment shall not be imposed.

Article 006

The Honest Bro

A Bro shall tell another Bro the truth, even when the truth is inconvenient. False reassurance is not loyalty.

Chapter III — The Code of Conduct

Article 007

The Ex Rule

A Bro shall approach another Bro's former relationship with caution, respect, and honest communication. No friendship is worth deliberate betrayal.

Article 008

The Invitation Principle

When a Bro extends a genuine invitation, another Bro should make a reasonable effort to attend or communicate honestly when unable to do so.

Article 009

The No Bro Left Behind Rule

A Bro shall make sure his fellow Bros are safe and accounted for before leaving a shared event.

"A Bro is a friend, a companion, and a brother." — The Principles of Brotherhood
