BOOK I — FUNDAMENTAL LAWS OF BROTHERHOOD

CHAPTER I — THE SUPREMACY OF BROTHERHOOD

ARTICLE 1 — BROS BEFORE HOES

Lex Prima — The Supreme Law

A Bro shall always prioritize the Brotherhood over temporary romantic interests.

No Bro shall abandon, neglect or betray another Bro solely for the purpose of pursuing a romantic interest.

No romantic relationship shall automatically supersede the obligations established by this Code.

A Bro shall not permit romantic infatuation to impair his judgment concerning matters of Brotherhood.

Any violation shall be subject to disciplinary review.

ARTICLE 2 — THE EX-GIRLFRIEND PROHIBITION

A Bro shall not engage in romantic or intimate relations with the former romantic partner of another Bro.

The prohibition shall apply regardless of the duration, seriousness or circumstances of the former relationship.

A Bro shall not circumvent this Article by claiming that the relationship was unofficial.

An exception may be granted only with the explicit and freely given consent of the affected Bro.

Consent shall not be presumed from silence, absence or failure to object.

The affected Bro shall retain the right to withdraw his consent before the commencement of such relationship.

ARTICLE 3 — THE BRO'S WORD

A Bro's promise to another Bro shall be considered binding.

No Bro shall knowingly make a promise he has no intention of fulfilling.

A Bro who becomes unable to fulfill an obligation shall notify the affected Bro within a reasonable period.

Deliberate deception concerning the fulfillment of a promise shall constitute a breach of Brotherhood.

The severity of the breach shall be determined in accordance with the circumstances.

ARTICLE 4 — THE PROHIBITION OF BRO BETRAYAL

No Bro shall knowingly expose another Bro to humiliation, embarrassment or reputational damage for personal entertainment.

A Bro shall not use confidential information entrusted to him as a means of manipulation.

No Bro shall deliberately sabotage another Bro's legitimate opportunities.

Any act of betrayal committed in the presence of third parties shall be considered an aggravating circumstance.

The affected Bro shall be entitled to request a formal hearing before the Brotherhood.

ARTICLE 5 — THE SACRED DUTY OF BRO ASSISTANCE

Every Bro shall make reasonable efforts to assist another Bro in a situation of genuine difficulty.

A Bro shall not abandon another Bro during a mutually agreed social outing without reasonable justification.

When a Bro requests assistance using the phrase "Bro, I need you", the request shall receive immediate consideration.

Assistance may include:

a. Providing a reasonable excuse;

b. Facilitating an exit from an uncomfortable situation;

c. Offering honest advice;

d. Protecting the dignity of the affected Bro.

No Bro shall be obligated to participate in unlawful, dangerous or harmful conduct under the pretext of Brotherhood.

ARTICLE 6 — THE LAW OF BRO CONFIDENTIALITY

Any personal information disclosed between Bros in confidence shall remain confidential.

No Bro shall disclose another Bro's secrets without permission.

Information obtained during private conversations shall not be used for public entertainment.

The obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination of a friendship.

Exceptions shall apply where disclosure is reasonably necessary to prevent serious harm.

ARTICLE 7 — THE PROHIBITION OF COCK-BLOCKING

No Bro shall deliberately interfere with another Bro's mutually welcomed romantic pursuit without legitimate justification.

Prohibited interference shall include:

a. Deliberately interrupting a meaningful conversation;

b. Spreading knowingly false information;

c. Inventing embarrassing stories;

d. Intentionally disrupting an agreed meeting.

A Bro shall be permitted to intervene where another person is uncomfortable, pressured or otherwise being treated improperly.

Unjustified interference shall constitute an offense against the Brotherhood.

ARTICLE 8 — THE RULE OF MUTUAL RESPECT

Every Bro shall treat fellow Bros with dignity and reasonable consideration.

No Bro shall exploit another Bro's weaknesses for personal advantage.

Disagreements shall not automatically constitute betrayal.

No Bro shall be required to tolerate repeated disrespect merely because the offender is a fellow Bro.

Brotherhood shall not constitute immunity from accountability.

ARTICLE 9 — THE RULE OF THE WINGMAN

Every Bro accompanying another Bro on a mutually agreed social occasion shall make reasonable efforts to support the latter's social confidence.

A designated Wingman shall not intentionally compete with the Bro he has agreed to assist.

A Wingman shall not misrepresent the character, intentions or circumstances of his fellow Bro.

A Wingman shall recognize that his primary responsibility is assistance, not personal advancement.

No Wingman shall be required to lie, deceive or pressure another person in fulfillment of this Article.

ARTICLE 10 — THE LAW OF THE UNANSWERED MESSAGE

A Bro receiving a message from another Bro shall make reasonable efforts to respond.

Messages containing urgent requests shall receive priority over ordinary conversation.

A Bro shall not deliberately ignore another Bro solely to create an appearance of superiority.

The absence of an immediate response shall not, by itself, constitute a violation where reasonable circumstances prevent communication.

ARTICLE 11 — THE PROHIBITION OF PUBLIC HUMILIATION

No Bro shall deliberately humiliate another Bro in public for the purpose of gaining social approval.

Embarrassing photographs, recordings or private messages shall not be distributed without appropriate permission.

Friendly teasing shall be permitted where all involved parties are comfortable with its nature.

The repeated continuation of a joke after an objection shall constitute a potential violation.

ARTICLE 12 — THE LAW OF THE LAST BRO

No Bro shall knowingly leave another Bro isolated at the conclusion of a mutually agreed social gathering where reasonable assistance can be provided.

Before departing, a Bro shall make reasonable efforts to establish that his fellow Bros have suitable arrangements for returning home.

No Bro shall pressure another Bro to remain at an event against his wishes.

The safety of every individual shall take precedence over the continuation of any social occasion.

ARTICLE 13 — THE DESIGNATION OF THE BRO

A Bro shall be recognized as an individual who has established mutual trust, loyalty and friendship with another member of the Brotherhood.

The designation of Bro shall not be granted solely on the basis of acquaintance, proximity or temporary social interaction.

No individual shall be entitled to demand the privileges of Brotherhood without accepting its corresponding responsibilities.

The designation shall remain valid until revoked in accordance with the provisions of this Code.

ARTICLE 14 — THE LAW OF THE BRO HANDSHAKE

A mutually established handshake between two Bros shall be regarded as a recognized symbol of Brotherhood.

No Bro shall deliberately betray the agreed sequence of a handshake.

A Bro shall not reveal a confidential handshake to an outsider without the consent of its creators.

In the event of a failed handshake, both Bros shall be entitled to repeat the procedure without penalty.

ARTICLE 15 — THE RULE OF THE THIRD WHEEL

A Bro shall make reasonable efforts not to impose himself upon a couple during a private romantic occasion.

A Bro who knowingly enters such an arrangement shall acknowledge his status as the Third Wheel.

The couple shall make reasonable efforts to ensure that the Third Wheel is not deliberately excluded or humiliated.

The Third Wheel shall retain the right to depart at any time.

No Bro shall be required to serve as a Third Wheel on consecutive occasions without his consent.

ARTICLE 16 — THE LAW OF THE GROUP CHAT

Every Bro shall respect the confidentiality of communications within the Brotherhood's designated group chat.

Screenshots of private conversations shall not be distributed outside the group without appropriate permission.

A Bro shall not intentionally remove another Bro from the group chat for trivial or retaliatory reasons.

Any removal shall be accompanied by a legitimate explanation.

The creation of a secondary group chat for the purpose of discussing another Bro shall not automatically constitute a violation, provided that it is not used for harassment or malicious deception.

ARTICLE 17 — THE LAW OF THE SHARED SECRET

A secret entrusted to a Bro shall remain the property of the individual who disclosed it.

The receiving Bro shall not claim ownership over such information.

A secret shall not become public knowledge merely because multiple Bros are aware of it.

The obligation shall remain in force even after a disagreement between the parties.

ARTICLE 18 — THE LAW OF PRIOR CLAIM

Where two Bros independently express romantic interest in the same individual, neither Bro shall automatically possess exclusive rights to pursue such individual.

The Bros concerned shall resolve the matter through honest communication.

No Bro shall deliberately conceal his intentions from another Bro in order to obtain an unfair advantage.

The wishes and autonomy of the individual concerned shall remain paramount.

No agreement between Bros shall create ownership or entitlement over another person.

ARTICLE 19 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE DOUBLE CROSS

A Bro shall not secretly pursue the romantic interest of another Bro while knowingly pretending to provide assistance.

Any Bro who develops such an interest shall disclose the conflict honestly.

Deliberate deception shall constitute an aggravating circumstance.

The affected Bro shall be entitled to request an explanation.

ARTICLE 20 — THE LAW OF THE FIRST DATE

A Bro who accompanies another Bro before a first date shall make reasonable efforts to ensure that the latter arrives prepared and on time.

No Bro shall deliberately provide false information concerning the date's location, time or arrangements.

A Bro shall not interfere with the date after its commencement unless reasonably requested or necessary.

Unsolicited advice shall be permitted only to the extent that it does not become persistent interference.

ARTICLE 21 — THE LAW OF THE POST-BREAKUP PERIOD

Following the termination of a romantic relationship involving a Bro, fellow Bros shall refrain from exploiting the immediate emotional circumstances for personal advantage.

No Bro shall deliberately encourage humiliation or retaliation against either former partner.

A Bro shall not disclose private details of the former relationship without legitimate justification.

The former partners shall be entitled to maintain independent friendships within the Brotherhood.

The provisions of Article 2 shall remain applicable.

ARTICLE 22 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE DESPERATION TEXT

No Bro shall encourage another Bro to send a message while the latter is visibly acting under unreasonable emotional pressure.

A Bro may recommend waiting until the following day before sending a message of significant personal importance.

No Bro shall fabricate messages or impersonate another individual.

Advice under this Article shall not be used to manipulate or control another Bro's personal relationships.

ARTICLE 23 — THE LAW OF THE WINGMAN'S PRIORITY

A Bro designated as Wingman shall not abandon his assigned responsibilities merely because an alternative romantic opportunity presents itself.

Where the Wingman wishes to withdraw, he shall notify the Bro whom he has agreed to assist.

A replacement Wingman may be appointed with mutual agreement.

The Wingman shall not disclose embarrassing information about the Bro he is assisting.

ARTICLE 24 — THE LAW OF THE FORMER WINGMAN

No Bro shall intentionally sabotage the romantic prospects of a former Wingman as an act of revenge.

Previous disagreements shall not automatically invalidate the obligations of basic respect.

The provisions of this Article shall apply regardless of the current status of the friendship.

ARTICLE 25 — THE PROHIBITION OF FALSE INTEREST

No Bro shall encourage another Bro to pursue an individual while knowingly possessing information that makes such pursuit clearly unwelcome.

A Bro shall not invent signs of romantic interest.

A Bro shall not misrepresent another person's statements or intentions.

The dignity of all individuals shall be protected under this Article.

ARTICLE 26 — THE LAW OF THE ROMANTIC CONFESSION

A Bro who intends to disclose significant romantic feelings concerning a person closely connected to another Bro shall exercise reasonable discretion.

Where the matter directly concerns an existing relationship within the Brotherhood, the affected Bro should be informed.

Such disclosure shall not entitle any Bro to dictate the romantic decisions of another individual.

No Bro shall be punished merely for developing feelings beyond his reasonable control.

ARTICLE 27 — THE RULE OF THE UNITED DEPARTURE

Bros attending a social gathering together shall make reasonable efforts to coordinate their departure.

No Bro shall deliberately leave another Bro without informing him.

Any Bro wishing to depart independently shall be permitted to do so.

The safety of all members shall take precedence over the preservation of group arrangements.

ARTICLE 28 — THE LAW OF THE PARTY INVITATION

A Bro receiving an invitation to a gathering intended for multiple Bros shall make reasonable efforts to communicate relevant information to the group.

No Bro shall deliberately exclude a fellow Bro solely to cause humiliation.

The host shall retain the right to determine the guest list.

No Bro shall enter a private gathering without the host's permission.

ARTICLE 29 — THE PROHIBITION OF SOCIAL SABOTAGE

No Bro shall intentionally ruin another Bro's social occasion for personal entertainment.

Such conduct shall include deliberate embarrassment, malicious interruption and the unauthorized disclosure of private information.

A legitimate warning concerning inappropriate or harmful conduct shall not constitute social sabotage.

ARTICLE 30 — THE LAW OF THE LAST SLICE

The final portion of shared food shall not be claimed without reasonable consideration for the other Bros present.

A Bro wishing to consume the final portion shall first establish that no other Bro has reserved or reasonably expected it.

Where two Bros simultaneously claim the final portion, the matter shall be resolved by mutual agreement.

No Bro shall be entitled to claim the final portion merely by virtue of seniority.

ARTICLE 31 — THE RIGHT TO HONEST ADVICE

Every Bro shall be entitled to request honest advice from another Bro.

The responding Bro shall not knowingly provide advice intended to cause harm.

A Bro shall retain the right to reject advice without being considered disloyal.

Disagreement shall not constitute an offense.

ARTICLE 32 — THE RIGHT TO PERSONAL SPACE

Every Bro shall retain the right to decline social invitations.

No Bro shall be required to disclose private reasons for declining an invitation.

Repeated pressure following a clear refusal may constitute a violation of this Code.

Brotherhood shall not eliminate individual autonomy.

ARTICLE 33 — THE RIGHT TO A FRESH START

A Bro who has committed a minor violation shall be entitled to request forgiveness.

The affected Bro shall retain discretion concerning whether to grant forgiveness.

A previously resolved offense shall not be repeatedly invoked solely to cause humiliation.

Serious or repeated violations may be considered during future disciplinary proceedings.

ARTICLE 34 — THE LAW OF THE INVITATION

A Bro receiving an invitation to a gathering shall be entitled to accept or decline without unreasonable pressure.

A Bro who accepts an invitation shall make reasonable efforts to attend at the agreed time.

A Bro unable to attend shall notify the organizer as soon as reasonably practicable.

No Bro shall deliberately accept an invitation solely for the purpose of preventing another Bro from attending.

The host shall retain final authority over the guest list.

ARTICLE 35 — THE LAW OF THE DESIGNATED WINGMAN

A Bro appointed as Wingman shall fulfill his responsibilities in good faith.

A Wingman shall not deliberately undermine the social confidence of the Bro whom he is assisting.

A Wingman shall provide reasonable assistance when requested.

A Wingman shall not fabricate romantic interest on behalf of another Bro.

The appointment shall terminate upon mutual agreement or when the circumstances no longer require such assistance.

ARTICLE 36 — THE LAW OF THE GROUP PHOTOGRAPH

No Bro shall publish an unflattering photograph of another Bro without reasonable permission.

A Bro shall be entitled to request the removal of a photograph that violates his reasonable privacy.

No Bro shall deliberately select the worst possible photograph of another Bro for the purpose of humiliation.

Photographs taken during private gatherings shall be treated with appropriate discretion.

ARTICLE 37 — THE LAW OF THE UNEXPECTED ENCOUNTER

When two Bros encounter one another unexpectedly in public, mutual acknowledgment shall be encouraged.

Neither Bro shall be obligated to interrupt an important personal engagement.

A Bro shall not deliberately pretend not to recognize another Bro solely to avoid embarrassment.

The customary greeting shall be determined by the established traditions of the individuals concerned.

ARTICLE 38 — THE LAW OF THE MUSICAL AUXILIARY

The individual controlling the music at a gathering shall exercise reasonable consideration for the preferences of the other Bros.

No Bro shall monopolize the music selection for the entire duration of an event without agreement.

A song selected by a Bro shall not be interrupted solely for the purpose of ridicule.

The right to select music shall rotate where no alternative arrangement has been established.

ARTICLE 39 — THE PROHIBITION OF UNDECLARED HOSTILITY

No Bro shall maintain deliberate hostility toward another Bro while falsely representing the relationship as harmonious.

A Bro who has a legitimate grievance shall make reasonable efforts to communicate it directly.

Disagreements shall not automatically constitute grounds for termination of Brotherhood.

Persistent dishonesty concerning a dispute may constitute an aggravating circumstance.

ARTICLE 40 — THE RIGHT TO A BRO HEARING

Any Bro accused of violating this Code shall be entitled to present his account of the relevant circumstances.

No disciplinary decision shall be made solely on the basis of an unverified accusation.

The accused Bro shall be permitted to identify relevant witnesses.

The hearing shall be conducted in a reasonable and impartial manner.

No Bro shall be required to accept a verdict based solely on popularity or seniority.

ARTICLE 41 — THE LAW OF THE APOLOGY

A Bro who has caused legitimate harm to another Bro shall make reasonable efforts to acknowledge the conduct.

An apology shall be genuine and shall not be used merely to terminate an uncomfortable discussion.

The acceptance of an apology shall remain at the discretion of the affected Bro.

Repeated apologies without corresponding changes in conduct shall not automatically satisfy this Article.

ARTICLE 42 — THE PROHIBITION OF RETALIATORY BETRAYAL

No Bro shall justify deliberate betrayal solely by claiming that another Bro committed an earlier violation.

Previous misconduct may be considered during disciplinary proceedings but shall not automatically excuse subsequent misconduct.

Disputes shall be resolved in accordance with Book IX.

ARTICLE 43 — THE LAW OF THE TEMPORARY SUSPENSION

Where a conflict becomes sufficiently serious, the affected Bros may agree to temporarily suspend certain social obligations.

Such suspension shall not authorize harassment, humiliation or malicious disclosure of private information.

The suspension may be terminated by mutual agreement.

Fundamental obligations concerning safety and respect shall remain in force.

ARTICLE 44 — THE GREAT BETRAYAL

The deliberate exploitation of a fellow Bro's trust for substantial personal advantage shall constitute a serious offense.

The following conduct may qualify as a Great Betrayal:

a. Knowingly pursuing a fellow Bro's former romantic partner in violation of Article 2;

b. Deliberately exposing confidential information to cause harm;

c. Knowingly sabotaging a fellow Bro's important opportunity;

d. Falsely accusing a Bro of misconduct;

e. Repeatedly violating an express agreement of Brotherhood.

The severity of the offense shall be determined according to intention, consequences and repetition.

No offense shall be considered proven without a reasonable examination of the available circumstances.

ARTICLE 45 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE FALSE WINGMAN

No Bro shall accept the designation of Wingman while secretly intending to undermine the Bro he is assisting.

A Bro who deliberately misrepresents his intentions shall be subject to disciplinary review.

The offense shall be considered aggravated where the deception results in significant personal or social harm.

ARTICLE 46 — THE LAW OF THE BETRAYED SECRET

Deliberate disclosure of a confidential matter entrusted in good faith shall constitute an offense against the Brotherhood.

The unauthorized distribution of private correspondence shall be considered an aggravating circumstance.

A Bro shall not avoid responsibility by claiming that the information had already been shared with one other person.

The obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination of the friendship.

ARTICLE 47 — THE PROHIBITION OF BRO IMPOSTURE

No individual shall falsely claim to represent the Brotherhood in order to obtain privileges or advantages.

No Bro shall impersonate another Bro in private correspondence.

The unauthorized use of another Bro's identity shall constitute a serious violation.

Consent-based impersonation for harmless entertainment shall not constitute an offense.

ARTICLE 48 — THE LAW OF THE REPEATED OFFENDER

A Bro who repeatedly violates the same Article may be subject to increased disciplinary measures.

Previous violations shall be considered only where they have been reasonably established.

The severity of any sanction shall remain proportionate to the conduct.

No Bro shall receive immunity merely because of seniority or popularity within the Brotherhood.

ARTICLE 49 — THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE BRO COUNCIL

The Brotherhood may establish a council responsible for interpreting and administering this Code.

The Council shall consist of Bros recognized by the members concerned.

No member shall possess unlimited authority over the Brotherhood.

Council decisions shall be subject to the principles of fairness, proportionality and transparency.

Members shall be entitled to challenge a decision where reasonable grounds exist.

ARTICLE 50 — THE JURISDICTION OF THE COUNCIL

The Council shall have authority to review alleged violations of this Code.

The Council may request statements from the Bros concerned.

The Council shall distinguish between deliberate misconduct, accidental violations and reasonable misunderstandings.

The Council shall not impose sanctions exceeding those authorized by this Code.

Every decision shall be accompanied by a brief explanation.

ARTICLE 51 — THE LAW OF THE UNANIMOUS VERDICT

A decision requiring unanimous approval shall not be considered adopted unless every eligible Council member agrees.

Abstentions shall not be interpreted as affirmative votes.

A Bro directly involved in the dispute shall not participate in determining the verdict concerning his own conduct.

Where unanimity cannot be achieved, the matter shall be referred to further discussion.

ARTICLE 52 — MINOR VIOLATIONS

Minor violations shall include accidental or low-impact breaches of this Code.

Such violations may result in:

a. A formal warning;

b. An apology;

c. A reasonable compensatory gesture;

d. Temporary suspension of a ceremonial privilege.

The sanction shall reflect the circumstances of the violation.

ARTICLE 53 — SERIOUS VIOLATIONS

Serious violations shall include deliberate acts of betrayal, repeated dishonesty and substantial breaches of confidentiality.

The Council may impose:

a. A formal reprimand;

b. Temporary suspension from Council privileges;

c. A requirement to make reasonable amends;

d. A temporary restriction on participation in Brotherhood ceremonies.

No sanction shall involve humiliation, coercion or physical punishment.

ARTICLE 54 — THE STATUS OF THE OUTCAST BRO

In exceptional circumstances, a Bro may be temporarily excluded from certain Brotherhood activities.

Such exclusion shall require a properly established serious violation.

The affected Bro shall receive notice of the reasons and an opportunity to respond.

The status shall be reviewed after a reasonable period.

Permanent exclusion shall require exceptional justification and a fair procedure.

ARTICLE 55 — THE RIGHT TO APPEAL

Every Bro subject to a disciplinary decision shall be entitled to appeal.

An appeal shall be submitted within a reasonable period following notification of the decision.

The appeal shall be reviewed by individuals who were not directly involved in the original decision.

The reviewing body may uphold, modify or overturn the original decision.

No Bro shall be punished for exercising his right to appeal in good faith.

ARTICLE 56 — THE LAW OF THE BRO FIST BUMP

A fist bump exchanged between Bros shall constitute a recognized gesture of mutual acknowledgment.

A Bro shall make reasonable efforts to complete a fist bump once initiated.

Deliberate refusal without reasonable explanation may be considered a minor breach of ceremonial etiquette.

A failed fist bump shall not constitute grounds for disciplinary proceedings.

ARTICLE 57 — THE LAW OF THE BRO NOD

The Bro Nod shall constitute a recognized non-verbal form of acknowledgment.

It may be used where verbal communication would be unnecessary or socially inconvenient.

A Bro shall not be required to explain the meaning of a Bro Nod to an outsider.

The interpretation of an upward or downward nod shall depend on the circumstances and established local tradition.

ARTICLE 58 — THE LAW OF THE LEGENDARY STORY

A story involving an extraordinary achievement by a Bro may be retold within the Brotherhood.

The facts of the story shall not be deliberately altered to cause harm to another individual.

Embellishment for humorous purposes shall be permitted where the audience reasonably understands the fictional nature of the embellishment.

No Bro shall claim another Bro's achievement as his own.

ARTICLE 59 — THE LAW OF THE BRO ANNIVERSARY

The date on which two individuals formally recognize one another as Bros may be celebrated as a ceremonial anniversary.

Participation shall remain voluntary.

No Bro shall be required to purchase an expensive gift.

The significance of the occasion shall derive from the Brotherhood itself rather than material expenditure.

ARTICLE 60 — THE FINAL LAW OF BROTHERHOOD

The purpose of this Code shall be to preserve trust, loyalty, mutual respect and the dignity of every Bro.

No provision shall be interpreted as authorizing harm, harassment, coercion or discrimination.

The spirit of Brotherhood shall prevail where a literal interpretation would produce an unreasonable result.

Every Bro shall remain responsible for his own actions, regardless of the provisions of this Code.

This Article shall constitute the final interpretative principle of the present Book.

ARTICLE 61 — THE RECOGNITION OF BROHOOD

Brotherhood shall be established through mutual trust, loyalty and voluntary recognition.

No individual shall acquire the status of Bro solely through repeated physical proximity to an existing member.

The granting of Bro status shall be determined by mutual recognition rather than unilateral declaration.

No Bro shall use his status to demand unquestioning obedience from another member.

Brotherhood shall be maintained through conduct and not merely through titles.

ARTICLE 62 — THE LAW OF THE ORIGINAL BRO

An Original Bro shall be recognized as an individual whose friendship predates the formation of the current Brotherhood.

Such status may carry ceremonial privileges but shall not grant immunity from this Code.

No Original Bro shall use seniority to justify betrayal or misconduct.

All Bros shall remain subject to the same fundamental laws.

ARTICLE 63 — THE PROHIBITION OF BRO POACHING

No Bro shall deliberately attempt to destroy an existing friendship between two other Bros for personal advantage.

The introduction of new friends into the Brotherhood shall not constitute a violation.

No Bro shall claim exclusive ownership over another Bro's friendship.

Attempts to isolate a Bro from his existing social circle through manipulation shall constitute a serious violation.

ARTICLE 64 — THE LAW OF THE NEW BRO

A newly recognized Bro shall be treated with reasonable respect and consideration.

New Bros shall be informed of the fundamental provisions of this Code.

Ignorance of this Code may be considered when determining the appropriate response to a minor violation.

Repeated violations following notification shall not be excused on grounds of unfamiliarity.

ARTICLE 65 — THE LAW OF THE BRO TITLE

The designation of Bro shall not be used as a means of manipulation.

A person shall not be required to accept the designation if uncomfortable with it.

The use of ceremonial titles such as Chief Bro, Supreme Bro or Legendary Bro shall be permitted for entertainment and organizational purposes.

No title shall supersede the fundamental rights of another individual.

ARTICLE 66 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE BRO'S SISTER

A Bro shall exercise particular discretion before pursuing a romantic relationship with the sibling of another Bro.

The affected Bro should be informed before any serious romantic pursuit develops.

No Bro shall deliberately conceal such a relationship from the affected Bro.

The sibling concerned shall retain complete autonomy over her romantic decisions.

This Article shall not establish ownership, veto rights or entitlement over any individual.

ARTICLE 67 — THE LAW OF THE BRO'S MOTHER

No Bro shall deliberately pursue the romantic interest of another Bro's parent for the purpose of humiliation or retaliation.

Any genuine relationship arising independently shall be treated as a private matter between the individuals concerned.

No Bro shall use familial relationships as a means of provoking another Bro.

This Article shall not authorize interference in consensual adult relationships.

ARTICLE 68 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE SECRET DOUBLE DATE

No Bro shall knowingly arrange a romantic encounter involving another Bro without disclosing material circumstances.

A Bro shall not misrepresent the identity, intentions or expectations of a participant.

Any double-date arrangement shall be based upon informed agreement.

Deliberate deception shall constitute a violation of Article 25.

ARTICLE 69 — THE LAW OF THE BRO'S CRUSH

A Bro who discovers that another Bro has developed romantic feelings for a particular individual shall exercise discretion.

No Bro shall deliberately pursue the same individual in secret while pretending to provide romantic advice.

Where competing interests arise, the Bros concerned should communicate honestly.

The individual concerned shall remain free to make an independent decision.

No Bro shall be required to conceal genuine feelings indefinitely.

ARTICLE 70 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE REBOUND BETRAYAL

A Bro shall not deliberately exploit another Bro's recent romantic separation.

A Bro shall not pursue a former partner of another Bro merely to provoke jealousy or humiliation.

The prohibition shall apply irrespective of whether the former partners remain in contact.

Any exception shall require consideration of the circumstances and the explicit consent required under Article 2.

ARTICLE 71 — THE LAW OF THE EXCEPTIONAL EX

The Council may recognize exceptional circumstances concerning Article 2.

Relevant considerations may include:

a. The duration of the former relationship;

b. The time elapsed since its conclusion;

c. The present relationship between the affected Bros;

d. Whether explicit consent has been given;

e. Whether the proposed relationship involves deception.

No exception shall be granted through pressure, intimidation or manipulation.

The decision shall respect the autonomy of all individuals involved.

ARTICLE 72 — THE LAW OF LEGENDARY PLANNING

A Bro organizing a significant event shall make reasonable efforts to inform the relevant members of the Brotherhood.

No Bro shall deliberately withhold essential information from a member who has been invited.

The organizer shall retain authority over the reasonable arrangements of the event.

Participation shall remain voluntary.

No Bro shall be penalized for declining an event for legitimate personal reasons.

ARTICLE 73 — THE LAW OF THE LEGENDARY ENTRANCE

A Bro arriving at a gathering may perform a ceremonial entrance appropriate to the occasion.

The entrance shall not intentionally disrupt an ongoing serious matter.

The Brotherhood may recognize particularly memorable entrances as Legendary Events.

No Bro shall be obligated to recreate a previous entrance.

ARTICLE 74 — THE LAW OF THE LEGENDARY STORY

A legendary story shall be considered an event of historical significance within the Brotherhood.

The original participants shall be recognized where reasonably possible.

No Bro shall knowingly attribute another Bro's achievement to himself.

Retellings may include humorous exaggerations, provided that they are not presented as verified facts where that distinction matters.

A legendary story shall not justify the disclosure of confidential information.

ARTICLE 75 — THE LAW OF THE GROUP TRIP

Bros undertaking a group trip shall establish reasonable expectations concerning accommodation, transportation and expenses.

No Bro shall knowingly impose undisclosed financial obligations upon another Bro.

Shared expenses shall be communicated transparently.

A Bro shall not abandon the group without reasonable notice where doing so could create a safety issue.

Every Bro shall retain the right to separate from the group when necessary.

ARTICLE 76 — THE LAW OF THE LAST-MINUTE CANCELLATION

A Bro cancelling an agreed event shall notify the other participants as soon as reasonably practicable.

Repeated cancellations without reasonable explanation may be considered a minor breach of social obligations.

Genuine emergencies shall constitute sufficient justification.

No Bro shall be required to attend an event merely to avoid disappointing the group.

ARTICLE 77 — THE PRINCIPLE OF LEGAL INTERPRETATION

Every Article shall be interpreted in accordance with its wording, purpose and surrounding provisions.

No Article shall be interpreted in isolation where another provision directly affects its application.

In cases of ambiguity, the interpretation most consistent with the fundamental principles of Brotherhood shall prevail.

No interpretation shall authorize conduct prohibited by the fundamental safeguards of this Code.

ARTICLE 78 — THE HIERARCHY OF LEGAL AUTHORITY

The provisions of Book I shall constitute the foundational principles of this Code.

No subsequent Article shall override Article 1 unless a formal amendment expressly provides otherwise.

Special provisions shall prevail over general provisions where they address the same matter more specifically.

Amendments shall not operate retroactively unless expressly agreed and reasonably justified.

No Bro shall claim exemption merely because an Article is inconvenient.

ARTICLE 79 — THE LAW OF UNFORESEEN CIRCUMSTANCES

Where a situation is not expressly regulated by this Code, the Council may determine an appropriate interpretation.

Such interpretation shall be consistent with the established principles of Brotherhood.

The absence of an express prohibition shall not automatically establish permission.

Any newly recognized rule shall be recorded as a supplementary provision.

No individual shall be penalized for an unforeseeable violation of a rule that had not been established or communicated.

ARTICLE 80 — THE LAW OF THE LOOPHOLE

No Bro shall deliberately exploit a technical omission in this Code to justify conduct that clearly violates its fundamental purpose.

The Council may close a recognized loophole through a formal amendment.

Such amendment shall specify the conduct regulated and the date on which it enters into force.

A loophole shall not be considered closed merely because a Council member dislikes the outcome.

All amendments shall be documented in the official register.

ARTICLE 81 — THE LAW OF THE UNWRITTEN BRO RULE

Established traditions of the Brotherhood may be recognized as supplementary customs.

Such customs shall not contradict any express provision of this Code.

No unwritten custom shall impose a serious obligation without reasonable notice.

The Council may formally incorporate a recognized custom into a future edition.

ARTICLE 82 — THE LAW OF THE EMERGENCY EXCEPTION

Where an emergency creates circumstances not reasonably anticipated by this Code, ordinary procedural requirements may be temporarily suspended to the extent necessary.

The protection of human life, safety and dignity shall take precedence over ceremonial or social obligations.

No Bro shall be punished for reasonably prioritizing an emergency over a social commitment.

The Council may review the circumstances after the emergency has concluded.

ARTICLE 83 — THE AMENDMENT PROCEDURE

Any Bro shall be entitled to propose an amendment.

Proposed amendments shall be submitted in writing.

Every amendment shall include:

a. The Article or Articles affected;

b. The proposed new wording;

c. The reasons for the amendment;

d. The date of submission.

The Council shall review the proposal.

An amendment shall enter into force only following the approval procedure established by the Brotherhood.

Every adopted amendment shall receive a unique identification number.

ARTICLE 84 — THE FINAL CONSTITUTIONAL DECLARATION

This Code shall serve as the official written record of the principles, customs and obligations of the Brotherhood.

Every Bro shall be encouraged to familiarize himself with its provisions.

No Article shall be used to justify cruelty, coercion, harassment or the violation of another person's autonomy.

The Brotherhood shall be maintained through mutual respect, honesty and voluntary loyalty.

The Code may be expanded, revised and supplemented as circumstances require.

ARTICLE 85 — THE LAW OF THE UNANSWERED CALL

A Bro receiving a telephone call from another Bro shall make reasonable efforts to return the call.

Repeated calls shall indicate an increased level of urgency, but shall not automatically constitute an emergency.

A Bro shall not deliberately ignore an urgent request for assistance merely because he is occupied with recreational activities.

Failure to return a call shall not constitute an offense where reasonable circumstances prevent communication.

ARTICLE 86 — THE LAW OF THE DOUBLE TEXT

No Bro shall be ridiculed solely for sending multiple consecutive messages.

A Bro shall not deliberately withhold a response to manipulate another Bro's emotions.

The number of messages sent shall not determine the importance of the friendship.

A Bro shall retain the right to communicate without being subjected to unnecessary judgment.

ARTICLE 87 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE SCREENSHOT BETRAYAL

No Bro shall distribute screenshots of private conversations without appropriate permission.

The prohibition shall apply to text messages, direct messages, voice messages and other private communications.

Screenshots submitted as evidence during a legitimate dispute shall be permitted where reasonably necessary.

No Bro shall alter a screenshot in a manner designed to misrepresent the original conversation.

Deliberate manipulation of digital evidence shall constitute a serious violation.

ARTICLE 88 — THE LAW OF THE GROUP CHAT SILENCE

A Bro shall not interpret silence in a group chat as automatic rejection or hostility.

A Bro shall not intentionally flood the group chat with irrelevant messages to prevent another Bro from communicating.

Important announcements shall not be deliberately buried under unnecessary conversation.

No Bro shall be removed from a group chat without a reasonable explanation where removal is intended as punishment.

ARTICLE 89 — THE LAW OF THE DIGITAL WINGMAN

A Bro assisting another Bro through digital communication shall provide honest and reasonable advice.

No Bro shall impersonate another Bro when communicating with a third party without explicit consent.

A Bro shall not send messages from another Bro's account without authorization.

Drafting a message for another Bro shall be permitted.

The final decision to send such message shall remain with its intended author.

ARTICLE 90 — THE LAW OF THE ONLINE PRESENCE

A Bro shall not deliberately misrepresent another Bro's online activity to cause embarrassment.

Online status indicators shall not be treated as conclusive evidence that a Bro is available.

No Bro shall demand continuous availability from another Bro.

The right to privacy shall remain protected regardless of digital communication habits.

ARTICLE 91 — THE THREE DEGREES OF BETRAYAL

Betrayal shall be classified into three degrees:

a. First Degree: Minor breach of trust;

b. Second Degree: Deliberate breach of a substantial obligation;

c. Third Degree: Severe betrayal involving significant and intentional harm.

The classification shall consider intention, consequences and repetition.

No Bro shall automatically be classified as a Third-Degree offender solely because of an accusation.

The classification shall be determined following a reasonable review.

ARTICLE 92 — THE LAW OF THE SECOND CHANCE

A Bro who has committed a violation shall be entitled to request an opportunity to restore trust.

The granting of a second chance shall remain at the discretion of the affected Bro.

A second chance shall not eliminate the historical record of the violation.

Repeated misconduct may constitute grounds for refusing further reconciliation.

No Bro shall be required to forgive conduct that has caused serious harm.

ARTICLE 93 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE FALSE APOLOGY

An apology shall not be considered genuine where it is made solely to avoid accountability.

Statements intended to shift responsibility entirely onto the affected Bro shall not satisfy this Article.

A Bro shall not demand immediate forgiveness following an apology.

Genuine reconciliation shall require appropriate consideration of the consequences of the original conduct.

ARTICLE 94 — THE LAW OF THE RETURNING BRO

A former member of the Brotherhood may request reinstatement following a period of separation.

Reinstatement shall require consideration of the original circumstances.

The affected members shall be entitled to express objections.

No Bro shall be required to resume a friendship against his wishes.

A returning Bro shall remain subject to all applicable provisions of this Code.

ARTICLE 95 — THE PROHIBITION OF PERMANENT GRUDGES

A Bro shall not deliberately maintain a minor disagreement solely to exercise social control over another Bro.

Resolved disputes shall not be repeatedly reopened without legitimate reason.

This Article shall not prohibit remembering serious misconduct or establishing reasonable boundaries.

Forgiveness and reconciliation shall remain distinct concepts.

ARTICLE 96 — THE CLASSIFICATION OF BRO INFORMATION

Information shared within the Brotherhood shall be assigned one of the following classifications:

a. Class I — Public Information;

b. Class II — Brotherhood Information;

c. Class III — Confidential Information;

d. Class IV — Highly Confidential Information.

The classification shall be determined by the person disclosing the information or by the reasonable circumstances of disclosure.

No classification shall be used to conceal misconduct or prevent someone from seeking necessary help.

ARTICLE 97 — CLASS I: PUBLIC INFORMATION

Public Information may be disclosed without special authorization.

Such information shall include publicly available facts and announcements.

No Bro shall falsely classify another individual's private information as Public Information.

ARTICLE 98 — CLASS II: BROTHERHOOD INFORMATION

Brotherhood Information shall be intended primarily for members of the Brotherhood.

Disclosure outside the Brotherhood shall require reasonable consideration of the circumstances.

Ordinary social plans and internal arrangements may be classified under this category.

ARTICLE 99 — CLASS III: CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information shall not be disclosed without the permission of the individual concerned.

Such information may include private relationship matters, personal difficulties and sensitive conversations.

Unauthorized disclosure may constitute a serious violation of Article 46.

ARTICLE 100 — CLASS IV: HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Highly Confidential Information shall receive the highest level of protection under this Code.

Disclosure shall require explicit permission, except where necessary to prevent serious harm or obtain appropriate assistance.

No Bro shall use Highly Confidential Information for entertainment, social advancement or personal revenge.

The obligation shall survive the termination of Brotherhood.

ARTICLE 101 — THE LAW OF FRIENDLY COMPETITION

Competition between Bros shall be permitted and encouraged where conducted in good faith.

No Bro shall deliberately sabotage another Bro's performance.

The outcome of a competition shall not automatically determine the social standing of its participants.

A Bro shall acknowledge a legitimate victory by another Bro.

The rules of any competition shall be established before its commencement.

ARTICLE 102 — THE LAW OF THE REMATCH

A Bro who loses a friendly competition may request a rematch.

The opposing Bro shall retain the right to accept or decline.

No Bro shall alter the rules after losing in order to invalidate the result.

A rematch shall be conducted under mutually agreed conditions.

ARTICLE 103 — THE PROHIBITION OF FALSE VICTORY

No Bro shall knowingly claim victory in a competition he has lost.

No Bro shall deliberately falsify the score or results.

A disputed result shall be reviewed according to the originally established rules.

The deliberate manipulation of a result shall constitute a violation of Brotherhood integrity.

ARTICLE 104 — THE LAW OF THE LEGENDARY CHALLENGE

A challenge may be recognized as Legendary where it demonstrates exceptional creativity, skill or commitment.

Participation shall remain voluntary.

No challenge shall require dangerous, unlawful or humiliating conduct.

The Brotherhood may formally recognize the winner through a ceremonial title.

No title shall grant authority over other members.

ARTICLE 105 — THE LAW OF THE HUMBLE VICTORY

A Bro who wins a competition shall refrain from deliberately humiliating the defeated participant.

Friendly celebration shall be permitted.

Repeated boasting intended to cause genuine distress may constitute a minor violation.

A defeated Bro shall not be required to pretend that the result was different.

ARTICLE 106 — THE LAW OF THE BRO TOAST

A ceremonial toast may be conducted to recognize a significant event within the Brotherhood.

Participation shall remain voluntary.

No individual shall be required to consume alcohol as part of a ceremonial tradition.

Non-alcoholic beverages shall be equally valid for the purposes of this Article.

The toast shall be conducted with reasonable respect for all participants.

ARTICLE 107 — THE LAW OF THE BRO AWARD

The Brotherhood may establish honorary awards recognizing exceptional conduct.

Such awards may include:

a. Bro of the Year;

b. Legendary Wingman;

c. Most Loyal Bro;

d. Supreme Problem Solver;

e. Keeper of the Bro Code.

Awards shall be honorary and shall not establish legal superiority over other Bros.

The selection procedure shall be established before voting begins.

ARTICLE 108 — THE LAW OF THE BRO ANNOUNCEMENT

A significant event concerning the Brotherhood may be formally announced.

Announcements shall not disclose private information without permission.

No Bro shall fabricate a significant event to obtain ceremonial recognition.

The announcement may be entered into the historical register.

ARTICLE 109 — THE LAW OF THE HISTORICAL RECORD

Significant events involving the Brotherhood may be recorded in an official historical register.

Records shall distinguish verified facts from humorous recollections.

No Bro shall knowingly alter historical records to falsely accuse another member.

Corrections may be made where inaccuracies are identified.

Private information shall remain protected under Book XVIII.

ARTICLE 110 — THE SEVEN DEADLY SINS OF BROTHERHOOD

The following conduct shall be recognized as the Seven Deadly Sins of Brotherhood:

Betraying a Bro for personal romantic advancement.

Deliberately pursuing a fellow Bro's former partner in violation of Article 2.

Exposing a confidential secret with malicious intent.

Abandoning a Bro in a genuine emergency.

Deliberately sabotaging a fellow Bro's significant opportunity.

Fabricating evidence to damage another Bro's reputation.

Repeatedly exploiting the trust of fellow Bros without remorse.

ARTICLE 111 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE UNJUSTIFIED ABANDONMENT

No Bro shall deliberately abandon another Bro in circumstances involving a genuine and foreseeable risk to safety.

A Bro shall make reasonable efforts to obtain appropriate assistance.

No Bro shall be required to place himself in danger where doing so would create an unreasonable risk.

This Article shall take precedence over ordinary social obligations.

ARTICLE 112 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE ULTIMATE BETRAYAL

The Ultimate Betrayal shall constitute the most serious category of offense recognized by this Code.

It shall require deliberate and substantial harm to the trust forming the foundation of the Brotherhood.

No accusation of Ultimate Betrayal shall be accepted without a reasonable examination of the circumstances.

The Council shall consider whether reconciliation remains possible.

No Bro shall be declared permanently unworthy of friendship solely by an arbitrary Council decision.

ARTICLE 113 — THE LAW OF THE BRO ABOVE ALL

Every Bro shall recognize that Brotherhood is founded upon mutual loyalty rather than blind obedience.

No Bro shall be required to support wrongdoing merely because another Bro requests it.

Honest disagreement shall be recognized as compatible with genuine Brotherhood.

The dignity and safety of every individual shall remain protected.

This Article shall govern the interpretation of all provisions concerning loyalty.

ARTICLE 114 — THE LAW OF PUBLIC LOYALTY

A Bro shall not deliberately humiliate another Bro in the presence of outsiders.

A Bro shall make reasonable efforts to preserve the dignity of fellow Bros during public disagreements.

Private disputes shall, wherever reasonably possible, be addressed privately.

No Bro shall be required to defend another Bro's dishonest or harmful conduct.

Public loyalty shall not supersede the obligation to act honestly.

ARTICLE 115 — THE LAW OF THE BRO INTRODUCTION

A Bro introducing another Bro to an unfamiliar individual shall make reasonable efforts to provide an appropriate introduction.

No Bro shall deliberately invent embarrassing personal information during such introduction.

A nickname may be used where commonly accepted by the individual concerned.

The introduced Bro shall retain the right to correct any materially inaccurate statement.

ARTICLE 116 — THE LAW OF PUBLIC EMBARRASSMENT

No Bro shall deliberately reveal an embarrassing personal incident involving another Bro for the purpose of obtaining social approval.

Stories previously agreed to be private shall remain confidential.

Friendly teasing shall be permitted where it remains mutually acceptable.

A Bro who has been asked to stop shall cease the conduct within a reasonable period.

Repeated violations may constitute harassment rather than ordinary joking.

ARTICLE 117 — THE LAW OF THE PUBLIC ARGUMENT

No Bro shall deliberately provoke another Bro into an argument in order to entertain spectators.

A Bro shall be permitted to disagree openly and respectfully.

Neither seniority nor popularity shall determine the correctness of an argument.

Serious disagreements shall be resolved in accordance with Book VII.

ARTICLE 118 — THE LAW OF THE BRO'S REPUTATION

A Bro shall not knowingly spread false information concerning another Bro.

Honest criticism shall not constitute a violation.

No Bro shall fabricate achievements, relationships or personal circumstances on behalf of another Bro without consent.

Deliberate reputational damage shall constitute an aggravating circumstance under Article 44.

ARTICLE 119 — THE LAW OF THE FIRST DECLARATION

Where a Bro openly declares romantic interest in an individual, fellow Bros shall exercise reasonable discretion before pursuing the same individual.

Such declaration shall not establish exclusive rights over the individual concerned.

No Bro shall deliberately conceal competing intentions.

The individual concerned shall retain complete freedom to determine her own preferences.

ARTICLE 120 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE SECRET PURSUIT

No Bro shall secretly pursue an individual while simultaneously pretending to assist another Bro in pursuing the same individual.

Such conduct shall constitute a breach of trust.

The offense shall be considered aggravated where false advice or fabricated information was used.

Honest disclosure of competing interests shall not constitute an offense by itself.

ARTICLE 121 — THE LAW OF THE FRIEND'S CRUSH

A Bro informed of another Bro's romantic interest shall exercise reasonable discretion.

No Bro shall intentionally ridicule another Bro for expressing genuine romantic feelings.

No Bro shall deliberately interfere with the opportunity of another Bro solely for amusement.

The individual concerned shall remain free to accept or reject any romantic interest.

ARTICLE 122 — THE LAW OF THE FORMER DATE

The prohibition established under Article 2 shall principally concern former romantic partners.

A casual acquaintance or unsuccessful first date shall not automatically qualify as a former relationship.

Nevertheless, a Bro shall exercise discretion where another Bro has expressed significant emotional attachment.

Deliberate deception shall remain prohibited regardless of the technical classification of the previous encounter.

ARTICLE 123 — THE LAW OF THE DOUBLE BRO BETRAYAL

A Double Bro Betrayal shall occur where two or more Bros knowingly coordinate conduct intended to deceive a third Bro.

Each participating Bro shall be individually responsible for his contribution.

The existence of multiple participants shall constitute an aggravating circumstance.

No Bro shall escape responsibility by claiming that another participant initiated the conduct.

ARTICLE 124 — THE LAW OF THE ROMANTIC WITHDRAWAL

A Bro may withdraw from a romantic pursuit at any time.

No fellow Bro shall ridicule such withdrawal where it is made in good faith.

A Bro shall not be obligated to continue pursuing an individual merely to preserve his reputation.

Withdrawal shall not automatically constitute a breach of the Wingman obligations.

ARTICLE 125 — THE LAW OF THE BORROWED MONEY

A Bro borrowing money from another Bro shall make reasonable efforts to repay the agreed amount.

The repayment date shall be established before or at the time of the loan.

Where repayment becomes impossible within the agreed period, the borrowing Bro shall communicate the circumstances honestly.

No Bro shall deliberately exploit friendship to avoid legitimate financial obligations.

The amount borrowed shall not determine the importance of the obligation.

ARTICLE 126 — THE LAW OF THE SHARED BILL

Where Bros agree to share expenses, each participant shall contribute according to the established arrangement.

No Bro shall knowingly impose an undisclosed expense upon another participant.

The existence of Brotherhood shall not create an automatic obligation to pay another Bro's personal expenses.

Disputed expenses shall be resolved through transparent discussion.

ARTICLE 127 — THE PROHIBITION OF THE FINANCIAL EXPLOITATION

No Bro shall repeatedly borrow money without making reasonable efforts to repay it.

A Bro shall not manipulate another Bro's loyalty to obtain financial advantages.

Deliberate misrepresentation concerning the purpose of a loan shall constitute an aggravating circumstance.

No Bro shall be required to lend money he cannot reasonably afford to lend.

ARTICLE 128 — THE LAW OF THE FREE OFFER

A Bro offering to pay for another Bro shall not automatically acquire the right to demand repayment.

Where repayment is expected, such expectation shall be communicated beforehand.

A gift shall not be retrospectively converted into a debt.

No Bro shall use a voluntary gift as a means of controlling another Bro.

ARTICLE 129 — THE LAW OF THE LAST COIN

No Bro shall knowingly take another Bro's money without permission.

Borrowed change shall be returned within a reasonable period.

Small amounts shall not become exempt from repayment merely because they are considered insignificant.

Repeated minor debts may be consolidated into one settlement.

ARTICLE 130 — THE PROHIBITION OF UNAUTHORIZED BORROWING

No Bro shall take another Bro's personal property without permission.

The phrase "We're Bros" shall not constitute sufficient authorization.

Borrowed property shall be returned in substantially the same condition, subject to ordinary wear.

Any accidental damage shall be disclosed promptly.

ARTICLE 131 — THE LAW OF THE BORROWED CLOTHING

A Bro borrowing clothing shall make reasonable efforts to return it clean and undamaged.

Clothing borrowed for a temporary occasion shall not be treated as permanently transferred.

No Bro shall claim ownership merely because the clothing has remained in his possession for an extended period.

The original owner shall retain the right to request its return.

ARTICLE 132 — THE LAW OF THE PERSONAL DEVICE

A Bro shall not access another Bro's personal device without permission.

Private messages, photographs and accounts shall remain protected.

No Bro shall deliberately inspect private communications merely because a device has been left unattended.

Emergency access may be justified where necessary to obtain assistance or prevent serious harm.

ARTICLE 133 — THE LAW OF THE LOST PROPERTY

A Bro discovering property belonging to another Bro shall make reasonable efforts to return it.

No Bro shall knowingly retain lost property while concealing its discovery.

The owner shall be notified where reasonably possible.

The discovery of valuable property shall not create ownership rights.

ARTICLE 134 — THE LAW OF THE SHARED SPACE

Bros sharing a residence or temporary accommodation shall respect each other's reasonable privacy.

Shared areas shall be maintained in a reasonably usable condition.

No Bro shall intentionally damage shared property.

Individual responsibilities shall be established through mutual agreement.

No Bro shall claim unrestricted authority over a shared space merely because he uses it more frequently.

ARTICLE 135 — THE LAW OF THE EMERGENCY CALL

A Bro receiving a credible emergency request shall make reasonable efforts to establish the nature of the emergency.

Where appropriate, the Bro shall contact emergency services or another qualified source of assistance.

A Bro shall not delay necessary assistance for the purpose of preserving a social arrangement.

No Bro shall be required to undertake an action presenting an unreasonable personal risk.

ARTICLE 136 — THE LAW OF THE SAFE RETURN

Bros participating in a shared outing shall make reasonable efforts to ensure that everyone can return safely.

No Bro shall pressure another Bro to travel with an unsafe or unsuitable driver.

A Bro shall not be ridiculed for choosing a safe means of transportation.

The availability of safe transport shall take precedence over convenience.

ARTICLE 137 — THE LAW OF THE UNEXPECTED ABSENCE

Where a Bro unexpectedly becomes unreachable during a situation presenting reasonable safety concerns, fellow Bros may make proportionate efforts to establish his well-being.

Such efforts shall respect reasonable privacy.

No Bro shall falsely report an emergency as a prank.

Deliberate false emergency reports may constitute a serious violation.

ARTICLE 138 — THE LAW OF THE DISTRESSED BRO

A Bro experiencing significant personal distress shall be treated with dignity and consideration.

Fellow Bros shall not dismiss genuine concerns merely because they are unfamiliar with the circumstances.

No Bro shall be required to provide professional assistance beyond his competence.

Appropriate outside help shall be encouraged where necessary.

Confidentiality shall not prevent seeking assistance when someone's safety is at risk.

ARTICLE 139 — THE PRINCIPLE OF EQUAL BROHOOD

Every recognized Bro shall be subject to the same fundamental principles.

No individual shall possess unlimited privileges by virtue of wealth, popularity, age or seniority.

Differences in personal circumstances shall be considered where relevant.

The Council shall apply this Code consistently.

ARTICLE 140 — THE PROHIBITION OF SELECTIVE LOYALTY

No Bro shall invoke Brotherhood only when doing so provides personal advantage.

The obligations of Brotherhood shall apply during both favorable and unfavorable circumstances.

A Bro shall not demand loyalty while consistently refusing to demonstrate it.

Repeated selective loyalty may constitute a breach of Article 3.

ARTICLE 141 — THE LAW OF THE BRO CODE ITSELF

Every Bro shall recognize this Code as a written framework governing the customs and obligations of the Brotherhood.

No Bro shall knowingly misrepresent an original provision.

No Bro shall claim that an invented rule was expressly established by Barney Stinson or the original television series.

Rules inspired by How I Met Your Mother shall be distinguished from original supplementary legislation.

The Code shall remain subject to expansion and revision.

ARTICLE 142 — THE LAW OF THE SUPREME BRO

The title of Supreme Bro may be awarded ceremonially to an individual recognized for exceptional loyalty and service.

The title shall not establish authority to override this Code.

The holder shall remain subject to every applicable provision.

The title may be revoked following a fair review of serious misconduct.

No individual shall be entitled to the title merely by declaring himself Supreme Bro.

ARTICLE 143 — THE LAW OF THE ETERNAL BRO

Brotherhood shall not be measured exclusively by the frequency of communication.

Temporary distance shall not automatically terminate an established friendship.

A Bro shall be permitted to pursue independent ambitions and personal relationships.

Genuine loyalty shall be demonstrated through conduct rather than constant availability.

This Article shall survive every subsequent amendment to the Code.

Article 144 — The Law of Suiting Up

A Bro shall never underestimate the power of a properly chosen outfit.

A Bro shall dress appropriately for the occasion.

A Bro who refuses to suit up shall not be forced, but shall be reminded of the opportunities he has voluntarily abandoned.

Article 145 — The Lemon Law

A Bro may establish a reasonable time limit for determining whether a date is going well.

Neither party shall be obligated to continue a date that is uncomfortable or unwanted.

The Lemon Law shall never be used as an excuse for disrespectful treatment.

Article 146 — The Hot-Crazy Principle

A Bro shall exercise judgment when choosing a romantic partner.

No mathematical theory, graph, or fictional scale shall override actual respect, compatibility, or common sense.

Any Bro using a chart to justify treating another person badly shall be subject to ridicule.

Article 147 — The Naked Man

No Bro shall be required to use a ridiculous romantic tactic.

Any tactic involving surprise, pressure, or unwanted exposure is prohibited.

The dignity and consent of all parties shall remain above every supposed legendary technique.

Article 148 — The Playbook

A Bro may maintain a collection of legendary stories, strategies, and social traditions.

No entry shall be considered law merely because it appears in a book.

The Playbook shall never be used to deceive, pressure, or humiliate another person.

Article 149 — The Definition of Legendary

A Bro shall strive to be remembered for loyalty, courage, humor, and good judgment.

Legendary status cannot be purchased.

Legendary status cannot be declared by the Bro himself.

Article 150 — The Legendary Entrance

A Bro entering a room shall acknowledge his fellow Bros.

An entrance shall be considered legendary only if it does not interrupt an emergency, an important conversation, or another person's moment.

Article 151 — The Story of the Night

Every legendary gathering may produce one official story.

The story shall be told truthfully, except where harmless exaggeration is clearly understood.

No Bro shall alter another Bro's story to make himself appear superior.

Article 152 — The Rule of the Callback

A legendary joke may be repeated in future gatherings.

A Bro shall not repeat a joke after it has become genuinely hurtful.

The Council may officially retire an exhausted joke.

Article 153 — The Hall of Fame

The Council may recognize Bros for extraordinary acts of loyalty.

Awards shall be based on actions, not popularity.

No Bro shall nominate himself.

Article 154 — The Right to Challenge

Every Bro has the right to challenge another Bro to a friendly competition.

Participation shall be voluntary.

The rules must be established before the competition begins.

Article 155 — The Rematch

A defeated Bro may request one rematch.

The victorious Bro may accept or decline.

No Bro shall claim a victory obtained through cheating.

Article 156 — The Rule of Humility

A Bro may celebrate his victory.

A Bro shall not humiliate the defeated participant.

A Bro who cannot accept defeat shall temporarily lose the right to brag.

Article 157 — The Shared Victory

When a team wins, all participating Bros shall receive recognition.

No Bro shall claim sole responsibility for a collective achievement.

The Bro who contributed the least shall not necessarily receive the loudest applause.

Article 158 — The Sacred Game

During an agreed competition, the rules shall be respected.

No Bro shall change the rules merely because he is losing.

The phrase "That doesn't count" shall require evidence.

Article 159 — The Right to Move On

Every Bro has the right to recover from a failed relationship.

No Bro shall be mocked for having genuine feelings.

The Council shall recognize that emotional recovery cannot be assigned a fixed deadline.

Article 160 — The Rebound Clause

No Bro shall knowingly exploit another person's feelings following a breakup.

A Bro shall be honest about his intentions.

No person shall be treated as a temporary replacement.

Article 161 — The Confession of Feelings

A Bro may confess romantic feelings to another person.

Such a confession shall not create an obligation to reciprocate.

A Bro shall respect the answer, regardless of whether it is favorable.

Article 162 — The Friend Zone Dispute

Friendship shall never be considered a debt owed in exchange for romantic interest.

A Bro may choose to remain friends or respectfully create distance.

No Bro shall insult another person for not returning his feelings.

Article 163 — The Post-Breakup Boundary

A Bro shall respect the privacy of former partners.

Private messages and personal information shall not be distributed for revenge.

The end of a relationship shall not cancel the obligation to behave with dignity.

Article 164 — The Brotherhood Across Distance

Physical distance shall not automatically terminate Brohood.

A Bro who moves away remains a Bro.

Reunions shall be treated as opportunities to restore the Brotherhood.

Article 165 — The Silent Support

A Bro need not always provide advice.

Sometimes listening is the most appropriate form of assistance.

A Bro shall not force another Bro to discuss something he is not ready to discuss.

Article 166 — The Right to Change

Every Bro has the right to mature and change.

Old traditions shall not be used to prevent personal development.

A Bro who chooses a different path shall not automatically lose his title.

Article 167 — The Unconditional Respect

Brotherhood shall not depend on wealth, popularity, appearance, or social status.

Every Bro shall be treated as an equal.

Disagreement shall not constitute betrayal.

Article 168 — The Emergency Brotherhood

In a genuine emergency, ordinary disputes shall be suspended.

A Bro shall prioritize safety and appropriate assistance.

No Council verdict shall prevent a Bro from seeking help when needed.

Article 169 — The Exception of Consent

No article concerning another Bro's personal relationships shall override the freely given consent of the people involved.

The Council shall consider honest communication before declaring a violation.

Silence shall never be interpreted as consent.

Article 170 — The Supreme Interpretation

The Bro Code shall be interpreted according to its fundamental purpose: loyalty, respect, honesty, and Brotherhood.

No article shall be interpreted to justify cruelty, coercion, humiliation, or dishonesty.

Where two articles conflict, the principle of mutual respect shall prevail.
